SUPPLY CHAIN & INVENTORY ANALYSIS
PROJECT INSIGHTS

DATASET OVERVIEW
----------------
Total Orders: 550
Suppliers: 9
Products: 30
Warehouses: 5
Order Date Range: 01-Jan-2025 to 24-Feb-2026


KEY KPIs
--------
Total Order Value: 465.43M
Total Pending Quantity: ~30K
Total Stock Level: ~87K


ORDER STATUS ANALYSIS
---------------------
Delivered: 375 orders (68.2%)
Partially Delivered: 99 orders (18.0%)
Cancelled: 38 orders (6.9%)
In Transit: 38 orders (6.9%)

Key Insight:
68.2% of orders were delivered successfully, while 18.0% were
partially delivered. Another 13.8% were either cancelled or still
in transit, highlighting opportunities to improve order fulfillment
and delivery performance.

PORTFOLIO SUMMARY
-----------------
Built an end-to-end Supply Chain & Inventory Analysis project using

Power BI dashboard to monitor order fulfillment, inventory levels,
pending quantities, supplier performance and delivery trends.


KEY BUSINESS FINDING
--------------------
The analysis showed that 68.2% of orders were delivered, while 18.0%
were partially delivered and 13.8% were cancelled or still in transit.
This indicates potential opportunities to improve fulfillment,
inventory planning and supplier delivery performance.
